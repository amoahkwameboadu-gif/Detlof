8. Publish Class Results

Enter the whole class's scores in one table. When you publish, every student in that class gets their own results filled in on their portal automatically.

  1. 1. Download the class list and score sheet

    A CSV with every student in the class and their credentials — one row per student per subject, with the score columns blank for you to fill in.

  2. 2. Fill in the scores and upload

    Open the sheet in Excel, Google Sheets or LibreOffice. Enter the Class Score (40) and Exam Score (60) columns and save it as CSV. Totals, grades and GPA are worked out for you.

    Choose a CSV to preview it here.

  3. 3. Send for academic review

    The class goes to the Academics Admin, who reviews it and publishes. Nothing reaches a student portal until it is approved.
